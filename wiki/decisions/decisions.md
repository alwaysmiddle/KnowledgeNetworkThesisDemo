# Decisions

A decision compares the ideas generated for a problem and records which direction was chosen — one idea, a combination of ideas, or parts of several. It links back to the parent problem and the ideas it evaluated.

Decisions are append-only. When a decision is revisited, record a new decision that supersedes the old one rather than rewriting it.
