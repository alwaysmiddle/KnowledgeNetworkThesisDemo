# Ideas

Each idea is a candidate solution to a specific problem. A problem can have many ideas.

An idea document contains three things, developed iteratively:

- **Idea** — what this approach proposes and why it might work.
- **Sketch** — the rough solution shape: what it includes, what it explicitly does not include, and how it would work at a high level. No implementation detail yet.
- **Research** — feasibility check: has this been done before, what are the risks, what is unknown, what would need to be true for this to work.

Sketch and research are not separate steps — they evolve together inside the same document. Revise the sketch when research reveals new constraints. Research deeper when the sketch raises new questions.

Each idea links back to its parent problem. When a set of ideas is ready to compare, they feed into a Decision.

---

## Idea document template

```
# Idea: [short name]

Parent problem: [link]
Status: draft | researched | accepted | rejected

## Idea
[What this approach proposes]

## Sketch
[Rough solution shape. What is in scope. What is explicitly out of scope.]

## Research
[Feasibility, risks, prior art, open questions]
```
